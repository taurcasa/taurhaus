# Team-comprehension view — synthesis v2 (after cross-judgment)

Round 1: two independent xhigh concepts (`concept-fable-the-site.md`,
`concept-astra-product-cutaway.md`). Round 2: each model judged the other
(`cross-judgment-fable.md`, `cross-judgment-astra.md`) under the operator's
genericity correction. This document supersedes synthesis v1.

**Owning v1's errors first, because both judges caught the orchestrator:**
Fable found an internal contradiction (v1 adopted a "landed" tint that
combines work and review one sentence after banning dimension-combining
tints). Astra found manufactured agreement (v1 listed "no manufactured
region completion" and "rejection is never an operator-decision" as
convergence while the Site proposed derived versions of both). Both were
real. The conflicts are adjudicated explicitly below.

## Settled by mutual concession (genuinely, this time)

1. **Anchor: "Declared scope" of the endeavor** — not "product". Regions
   are whatever the addressed headings declare: capabilities, research
   questions, rubric cases. Scope stays still while work moves through it.
2. **Scope source: addressed scope sections in the team's designated
   planning document** (BRIEF.md default) — `[S2] Run work` headings plus
   `Scope: S2` lines in task descriptions (multiple references allowed;
   totals deduplicated by task id). Fable conceded `scope.yaml`; Astra
   conceded a **named scope owner** is required (the endeavor's designated
   owner — architect on a build, lead on a sweep) and that unknown IDs stay
   *unresolved references* until that owner adopts them: a typo must never
   promote itself into apparent scope.
3. **The walk ships** (Astra conceded the session-only notice: "an
   insufficient destination for a returning operator"). Bounded (≤6 lines),
   fixed severity classes, explicit fold count, template-compiled — **no
   generated prose in v1** — every line citing a record, rendered as a strip
   over the always-visible map, collapsing to one line when caught up.
4. **Lit change edges + full roster rail** (Astra stole both verbatim).
5. **Intervention transport: the member's own pane**, with a prefilled
   draft in the team's `ACTION REQUIRED:` convention from the selected
   evidence. The view reflects only ledger-recorded outcomes. Inline nudge
   only if recipient, delivery/failure, and duplicate-dispatch protection
   are all established — "the wire is trivial" is insufficient.
6. **Unplaced-work shelf with its fraction scoped to the endeavor's open
   tasks** (Fable's attack: an all-history fraction becomes wallpaper and
   the drift signal rots). History folds.
7. **Three-way freshness split** everywhere: read freshness ≠ source
   modification time ≠ recorded event time. **Ruling supersession is
   explicit**: a newer accept never silently closes an earlier reject.
8. **Degenerate mode is the intended mode for small endeavors**: no
   addressed headings → evidence strip + walk, one dismissible adoption
   prompt, zero nagging. A two-hour sweep never pays scope ceremony.
9. May-say / cannot-establish source table (Astra's) is the review
   checklist for every rendered state; activity honesty inherited from
   `activitySignal` verbatim; assignment and observed activity are separate
   claims.

## The three adjudicated conflicts

**A. Region lifecycle state.** Fable's revised position: one dimension per
visual channel (tint=work, flag=review, chips=observation). Astra holds
that even a work-only "settled" tint over-claims: all *mapped* tasks
complete cannot assert the scope is fulfilled (the never-tasked
password-recovery case), and hover cannot undo a five-second-scan meaning.
**Ruling — both are right; drop derived finished-ness from the preattentive
layer entirely.** The preattentive layer answers what it can honestly
answer: where work is concentrated (chip density + active ground
treatment), what changed (lit edges), where disputes are (flags). "All n
mapped tasks complete" is a count line, not a tint. The quiet manila
"filed" treatment exists but is **declared-only**: it renders exactly when
a source explicitly records the scope item's outcome (the scope owner marks
it in the planning doc). Derived at-a-glance progress is the one claim
neither concept could make honest, so no pixel makes it.

**B. The walk's absence contract.** Fable holds the durable read-mark (the
overnight absence is the field test's actual wound; session-local fails it
by construction). Astra holds the evidence boundary: an event-time filter
hides late-arriving records forever, reopened tasks vanish without
transition retention, and dwell-acknowledgement fakes reading. **Ruling —
durable walk on Astra's terms:** the read-mark filters on **first-observed
time** from a small app-local observation journal (record id/seq +
first-seen timestamp — taurhaus's own retained history, no team ceremony),
which covers late arrivals and reopens; acknowledgement is **explicit
only** ("mark caught up" — no dwell); acknowledging never clears flags or
resolves anything; the walk states its window and its fold. Acceptance
test: survives app restart and an overnight absence without lying about
either.

**C. "Needs you".** Astra's attack stands: a rejection with an owner
already correcting it is not an operator decision; in a retro, historical
rejections are the material, and deriving interrupts from them manufactures
noise exactly when the program works. **Ruling — v1 renders conditions,
never requests.** Flags say "contested" / "stalled (observation)" with the
responsible owner attached, scoped by the ruling's subject so verdicts
about assessed historical work never flag as current disputes. The
"needs you" category — and any badge counting it — waits for the explicit
typed operator-request contract (recipient, question, choices), a later
convention both concepts independently demanded.

## v1 scope (one contained feature PR, after the wave-1 retro)

Fixed regions from addressed headings in the designated planning doc;
`Scope:` references; chips + roster rail; lit edges; contested/stalled
condition flags with owners; the durable walk over the observation journal;
unplaced shelf (endeavor-scoped fraction); source-freshness bar; inspection
drawer deep-linking into existing surfaces; draft-beside-pane handles;
declared-outcome manila. Omitted: all inference, architecture edges, the
operator-request contract, milestone overlays, time scrubbing, any ledger
mutation, generated prose.

Field test (merging both judges' versions): after a real absence, the
operator must identify what changed, which scope parts are contested and
who owns them, the evidence behind one acceptance, and what remains
unknown — in thirty seconds — then act on one issue without losing the
scope context, and correctly name one thing the view does NOT claim.
