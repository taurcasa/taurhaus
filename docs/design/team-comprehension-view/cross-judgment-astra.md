**Cross-judgment — Astra’s revised position**

The Site wins on the operator’s return to work. It loses where it turns task records into claims about finished scope or required intervention. Adopt its arrival experience; tighten what that experience is allowed to say.

**1. STEALS — strongest first**

1. **“Thirty-second reabsorption test.”** Adopt verbatim as the primary field test. My brief-glance test underweighted the operator’s absence: recognizing assignments is easier than recovering what changed and deciding where to look next. Test reabsorption against known changes, including whether the operator recognizes missing evidence.

2. **“No line is generated prose in v1.”** Adopt verbatim, together with the bounded, evidence-linked walk and explicit overflow count. This beats my single session notice when several consequential events compete for attention. The cap limits presentation; it must not imply that six lines constitute a complete account.

4. **“Every member, including idle and offline ones.”** Adopt verbatim for the roster rail. My region annotations and unplaced-work shelf do not adequately account for people without current assignments. A complete roster prevents an absent chip from becoming an unexplained disappearance.

**2. ATTACKS — most consequential first**

1. **“Landed” is a false conclusion, even under Fable’s strict rule.**  
   Six mapped tasks complete and no open contested ruling establish those task facts. They do not establish that the declared scope is fulfilled. A greenfield authentication area can meet that rule with password recovery never tasked. A research question can have every search completed and remain unanswered. An evaluation can finish with a damning result. Even 100% task mapping cannot reveal work that was never represented as a task. The adjacent fraction and hover citation do not repair the region’s stronger visual claim. Replace “landed” with visible task-level evidence; show a scope outcome only when a source explicitly declares it.

2. **A timestamp and a signature do not make an honest absence account.**  
   A ruling dated 13:50 arrives after the operator acknowledges through 14:00. Filtering by event time can hide it forever. A completed-then-reopened task can vanish from a current-state comparison if transitions were not retained. These are failures of event coverage, untouched by template-generated sentences. Advancing the read-mark through dwell compounds the problem: time spent looking at one block does not establish that folded events were read. Require a defined history and observation contract, disclose gaps, and make acknowledgement explicit. Acknowledging a walk must never resolve an issue.

3. **“Needs you” mistakes negative evidence for a request.**  
   A reviewer rejects a packet; its owner is already correcting it. The Site nevertheless counts an operator decision. During a retro, historical rejections may be the intended material of the investigation, so the same rule manufactures interruptions precisely when the program is progressing. Staleness and an idle dependency owner also do not identify who must act. Until a source explicitly addresses the operator, show the recorded condition and responsible owner. Keep reject and oversize distinct, and keep both separate from operator requests.

4. **“Near-zero marginal ceremony” counts keystrokes and ignores decisions.**  
   A reproducibility task supports three research questions; one `area` token cannot express the association. A retro team has no architect; nobody inherits the proposed scope-file obligation. A misspelled ID creates a ghost “proposed area,” promoting a tagging error into apparent scope. The maintenance cost is deciding boundaries, shared membership, and changes—not typing the tag. Require an identified scope owner, permit multiple explicit references, and render unknown IDs as unresolved references until that owner adopts them. Task-mapping coverage measures association coverage, not the map’s overall trustworthiness.

**4. GENERALITY VERDICT**

**The merged v1 survives as a structure, but fails as written.** Stable declared scope, explicit associations, a walk, an evidence drawer, and existing intervention surfaces work across endeavors. Product language and inferred outcome states do not.

| Endeavor | What breaks in the concepts and merged v1 | Smallest fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Greenfield product build** | Component-shaped regions fit, but completed mapped tasks can make an incomplete capability appear landed. A region without tasks can appear unstarted despite work existing elsewhere. | Keep declared capabilities or subsystems. Show “mapped tasks complete” and “no linked task evidence.” Require explicit, appropriately scoped evidence before asserting a capability outcome. |
| **Research sweep answering open questions** | “Intended behavior,” architecture layers, commits, and demonstrations are poor defaults. A completed investigation may leave its question unresolved. A long experiment without ledger movement need not be stalled. | Let regions name declared questions or hypotheses and show their question text. Use linked studies, notes, datasets, or findings as available evidence. Keep task completion separate from any explicitly recorded answer; label inactivity as an observation unless a source establishes a blocker. |
| **Evaluation or retro grading past work** | “Landed” and rejection flags confuse completion of the assessment with quality of the assessed work. An accepted report may document a failed implementation. Historical rejects can become false current disputes or operator requests. | Let regions name declared evaluation cases, questions, or rubric dimensions. Keep each verdict’s subject visible: assessed work versus assessment artifact. If the subject is missing, say so. Surface current assessment assignments and explicitly addressed requests. |

Both concepts should use **“Declared scope”** at the top level. My “Product Cutaway,” capability-first language, and architecture-to-implementation depth cannot serve as universal conventions; those remain applicable to product endeavors. The generic depth is scope → associated work and evidence → source.

Replace the required behavior sentence with the declared objective, question, or criterion. Replace mandatory architect ownership with the endeavor’s designated scope owner. No separate lifecycle for each endeavor is necessary: work, review, and observation remain useful independent dimensions.

**5. ONE THING THE SYNTHESIS ITSELF GETS WRONG**

**It manufactures agreement.** Its “settled” convergence says there is no manufactured region completion and that rejection is never inferred to be an operator decision. The Site explicitly proposes both a derived “landed” state and rejection-derived “needs you” flags.

Those are unresolved design conflicts. Recording them as independent agreement lets contradictory behavior enter the same implementation under an apparent mandate. Rewrite them as explicit decisions, with the allowed claim and its required evidence stated before calling the merged v1 settled.
