## Why (measured, P from the threads study on the wave-1 archive)

Fifteen onboarding bodies were 39.8% of all retained inbox text; 94.3% of
every exactly duplicated character (53,508 of 56,768) was onboarding
replayed to the SAME seat — three to five copies of ~5,200-character
cards. Some replay after a fresh context or relaunch is necessary; the
snapshot cannot say which of the ten extra copies were wasted. The wave-2
review keeps the item and adds correctness: the first non-default-root
launch failed despite account selection, so a root or instruction change
needs a NEW operative card or a bounded correction, not suppression; the
census must separate necessary fresh-context recovery from same-context
replay; inspect delivery receipts and never count generated bytes as
consumed bytes. The wave-2 seats want the card's four earned reads (cold
resume, moved tip / crossed instruction, wait release, closure recovery)
plus cross-owner handoff — and "on the card" never substituting for
"delivered".

## Design dimensions (decide each with reasons; label I)

1. **Identity tuple:** team incarnation, member incarnation, context
   generation (fresh launch, resume, relaunch, post-compaction), role
   template revision, contract/packet revision, resolved account root and
   teams root — what changes produce a new card version versus a bounded
   correction versus nothing.
2. **Delivery rule:** once per context generation; what a nudge or a
   deadline reminder carries instead (a delta or a pointer, never the
   card); what a resume/relaunch delivers; what compaction delivers; how a
   correction supersedes (names the instruction it replaces — the
   threads study's contradiction row).
3. **The recovery card's contents** for the four earned reads: current
   assignment token and wait state, resolved roots, code/rubric tips, the
   restart cursor, the applicable corrections since the last card — and
   what it must NOT carry (peer verdicts, private bodies, doctrine that
   lives in linked documents).
4. **Receipts:** what "delivered" means per path (inbox record, hook
   stdout, tmux injection) using the overhaul study's receipt vocabulary;
   generated bytes are never consumed bytes.
5. **Ownership split:** what taurhaus renders (role/contract card), what
   mesh delivers (assignment/wait state), and where the version lives so
   both agree without a new store or watcher.
6. **Role-text delta** under the attention-budget rule (a handful of
   rules at most; point at the card, not at the studies).
7. **Measurement plan:** same-seat replay census on the wave-2 archive
   when it exists (gated), separating necessary recovery from replay, with
   the threads study's estimator disclosed and the 28,916.50 TE fixed
   onboarding baseline named; what can be measured on wave 1 now.
8. **Open questions** for the architect charter, each with a safe default.

## Not building
