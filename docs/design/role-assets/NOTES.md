# taurhaus role image assets

I recommend direction B, layered manila paper. Its broad silhouettes remain readable at small sizes, while paper grain, folded edges and shallow shadows provide richness for 128–256px catalog cards. The material connects directly to taurhaus’s manila-folder identity, and the restrained teal/manila palette feels calm alongside the desktop interface. A shared front-facing composition and a limited number of layers give the ten different role symbols a coherent visual language.

## Candidate directions

- A: duotone manila engraving on dark teal.
- B: tactile layered manila and teal paper (recommended).
- C: low-key sculptural ceramic studio objects.

## Generation record

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool, one call per asset. Each candidate is a separate generated PNG. Source exports are 1254 × 1254 pixels. The final set uses the two B candidates as material/style references.
